PWA Service Worker Cache Hardening
Full app shell cached on first load. Android Chrome + iOS Safari "Add to Home Screen" installs as standalone PWA with offline capability. No app store required.
🔴 Critical
libsodium.js End-to-End Encryption
WebAssembly port of NaCl. X25519 key exchange — private keys never leave device (IndexedDB only). Symmetric session key via ECDH. All cloud sync payloads and P2P message content encrypted.

Autosave every 10 minutes (soft toast). On disconnect: immediate cloud save + local download prompt. On graceful end: session summary (feelings, spars, oracle calls, pulse) → encrypted JSON export.

🟡 PHASE 3 — FEELING INTELLIGENCE 2.0 Guided flow · Journaling · Safety prompts

A complete redesign of the feeling-setting flow into a guided, empathic, branching conversation. The most psychologically rich phase.

Guided Feeling Flow (Branching Modal)
Step 1: Feeling wheel (or "good/bad?" for the stuck). Step 2: What caused this? Step 3 (negative): What would you like them to know? Step 4: Would you like to talk about it? Step 5: What would help you feel safe? Every step skippable — never an interrogation.

Feeling Validation Reminders Throughout
On open: "Your feelings are valid, always." Per feeling: a brief affirming sentence. On negative: "Nothing you feel is too much or wrong." On not sharing: "That's okay. Your feelings are expressed on your terms."
