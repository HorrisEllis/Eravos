Eravos
Built for the distance between you — a private, self-hosted space for two people to actually understand each other

Not a habit tracker with a couple's skin on it. Not a chatbot pretending to be your relationship. Eravos is a co-creation space — relational intelligence, not relationship surveillance — for two people who want to grow closer on purpose, together, without a company reading their feelings. Everything here syncs directly device-to-device, so it works exactly the same whether you're across the room or across an ocean — distance was never the thing standing between understanding each other.

Us vs. the problem — never each other

Most apps built for couples quietly put you on opposite teams — who logged in more, who initiated less, a scoreboard dressed up as insight. Eravos is built on the opposite premise: the two of you are never the problem. Whatever's actually hard — a rupture, a pattern, a need that's gone unspoken — that's the thing you're both working against, together. Nothing here is designed to keep score.

🕊
Nudge, never demand
Eravos suggests. It never insists. Every prompt is skippable, every share is optional, every "no" is a complete answer.
🔒
Solo until invited
The whole app works alone first. Nothing about you is shared until you choose to share it — and choosing later never erases what came before.
🏠
Self-hosted, actually private
Runs on your own machine. Your data lives on your device, not a company's server. No ads, no analytics, nothing to sell.
