⚖ WHY THIS ORDER

Everything asked for — the popup composer, the add-menu, shared cursors, the shared grammar — sits on two things that did not exist. A message engine not owned by a surface: a popup that owns its own thread means two stores that disagree the first time both surfaces are open. And an option primitive that always offers "Other": every prompt that would feed the grammar presented a closed list, so building the grammar first means the grammar only contains words a stranger chose. That is why A and B come before C. Not caution — the only order in which the later phases don't have to be rebuilt.

Every prompt, check-in and reminder has a real skip path. No feature blocks the rest of the app. No decline is re-asked more than once a day. No relational tool activates without both people opting in.

Understanding without labels

Gender, orientation and values fields always accept free text over a closed list.

Always self-hosted

No infrastructure this app depends on runs on a third party's server, as a standing default. Applies to anything added later, not just what is already decided.

One write authority per field

No two devices ever race to write the same piece of state.

Ephemeral means ephemeral

Cursors and typing are never written. Not capped — never written.

Two-sided by construction

Shared grammar, cursor sharing, read receipts: one person cannot turn them on for both.

Signal, not diagnosis

The system records what someone said they meant. It never infers a "real" meaning behind their words.
