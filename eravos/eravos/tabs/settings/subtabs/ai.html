🤖 AI ASSISTANCE

Eravos's AI has one job: helping the two of you communicate and understand each other better. It teaches technique instead of ghostwriting your words, and it never decides things for you. Everything in Eravos works fully without it — AI just makes some responses richer. Turn it off entirely with the master switch below.

Routes AI features through a real, logged-in provider session (Claude.ai by default) running in the desktop app — no API key stored here. If nothing's connected, every AI feature already falls back to curated offline guidance; this just makes those responses richer.
