⚠ PARTNER ACCOUNT (admin)

Inviting, redeeming an invite, decoupling, and deleting your own account live in Settings > System > Account & Partner now — those are things about your own account, reachable either role. This card keeps the one action that's genuinely admin: removing the other person's account from this device. Every button here still takes a full export first.

Remove deletes their account and their data from this device.

🔗 Relay (advanced)

By default the relay is derived from the page you are on — same host, same port, /relay. That is correct for one-port serving and cannot drift from the server. Only touch this if the relay genuinely lives somewhere else.
