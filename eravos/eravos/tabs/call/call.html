ℹ HOW IT WORKS

Uses the same P2P connection as the rest of the app — no third-party servers involved once connected. Both partners need to be online and connected for calls to work. Calling sends a request first — your partner gets 30 seconds to accept or decline before your camera/mic ever turn on. Your browser will ask for camera/mic permissions once they accept. Screen sharing works during video calls — your screen and camera bubble are sent as one feed, and there's a fullscreen button for couch-party moments. Transcription is local to each device (your mic only) and syncs so you both see one shared transcript — every line feeds the Lattice, and from there the Mythos.
