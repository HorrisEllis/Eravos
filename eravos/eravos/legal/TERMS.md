# Terms

Last updated: 1 August 2026 · Eravos v0.16.0

Plain words, because a document nobody reads protects nobody.

## 1. What Eravos is

Software you run yourself. It stores your writing on your own device and, if
you connect with someone, passes messages between the two of you through a
relay you host.

**There is no Eravos company.** Nobody operates a service for you. There is no
account on a server, no subscription, and nobody to email if it breaks. This
matters legally and practically: obligations that would normally sit with a
provider sit with you, because you are the provider.

## 2. What you are agreeing to

That you understand the above. That you are old enough to enter an agreement
where you live. And that what you do with it is yours.

## 3. What it is not

**Not a therapist, counsellor, or crisis service.** Nothing here is clinical
advice, and nothing it shows you is a diagnosis of you, your partner, or your
relationship. If something in it prompts you toward help, that prompt is a
door, not an assessment.

**Not a safety device.** Do not rely on it to reach someone in an emergency.
It runs between two devices over a connection that can fail, and it will fail
sometimes. In an emergency, use a phone.

**Not evidence.** Records here are yours and editable by you. Do not treat
them as proof of anything for anyone else.

## 4. Two people

Anything shared requires both of you to have opted in. That is enforced in the
software, not just promised here: a pairing code has to be scanned *and*
confirmed, shared grammar takes hold only when the other person takes it up,
and desires stay sealed until you have both answered.

**You are each responsible for what you send.** The software does not moderate
you, and it will not stop you being cruel to someone. If you are being harmed,
that is not a software problem and Eravos cannot fix it — Settings has
resources, and they are there whether or not the app has noticed anything.

## 5. Your data, your device

Everything lives on your device by default. Nobody else can read it, including
us, because there is no us with any access. Deleting the app or clearing the
browser deletes it. **There is no backup you have not made yourself.**

Export is in Settings, works at any time, and runs automatically before
anything destructive.

## 6. If you host the relay

You are running server software. That makes you responsible for it — keeping
it patched, its TLS valid, and its access controlled. If you expose it to the
internet, you have made a machine on your network reachable from outside, and
you should understand what that means before doing it.

## 7. No warranty

Provided as-is. No guarantee it works, keeps working, keeps your data, or
delivers a message. Given the whole point is a private conversation, **assume
loss is possible and export what matters to you.**

## 8. Liability

To the extent the law where you live allows: no liability for loss of data,
loss of a relationship, distress, or anything else arising from use of this
software. Where your local law does not permit that exclusion, your local law
wins — nothing here removes a right you have that cannot be signed away.

## 9. Changes

The version at the top is the one you agreed to. If terms change materially
you will be asked again rather than assumed to agree. **A changed document
you were never shown is not consent.**

## 10. Ending it

Stop using it. Delete it. Settings › Admin removes everything from your device
and exports first. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because there is nowhere
else.
